Case studies

Enterprise-style dashboard concepts

These remain brand-inspired samples for organizations similar to Merck, Nike, Wayfair, ShipBob, and Costco, so the portfolio stays professional and safe for public publishing.

Merck-style · Commercial + compliance

Pharma field performance cockpit

An executive cockpit tying territory sales, physician engagement cadence, product mix, sample utilization, and compliance monitoring into one leadership view.

Audience: Commercial director, regional heads, compliance lead.
KPIs: Rx growth, reach/frequency attainment, sample ROI, active prescriber coverage, compliance issue rate.
Depth: Territory drill-through, product segmentation, exception flags for high-risk territories.
Nike-style · Omnichannel retail

Global demand and inventory command center

A leadership dashboard connecting sell-through, stock health, markdown risk, return behavior, and campaign lift across channels and regions.

Audience: Merchandising VP, digital commerce head, inventory planner.
KPIs: Revenue, sell-through %, weeks of cover, return rate, full-price mix, stockout loss estimate.
Depth: Plan vs actual pacing, assortment drill, regional risk segmentation.
Wayfair-style · Marketplace + CX

Category margin and customer experience suite

A suite that lets category and operations leaders monitor contribution margin, fulfillment cost, supplier issues, returns, and customer satisfaction by category.

Audience: GM, category lead, CX operations.
KPIs: Contribution margin, AOV, cancellation %, damage rate, OTIF, NPS.
Depth: Supplier heatmaps, return root-cause patterns, cohort lens for repeat buyers.
ShipBob-style · Fulfillment operations

Warehouse network executive control tower

An operations control tower focused on throughput, backlog, labor output, accuracy, SLA risk, and carrier performance across a distributed node network.

Audience: COO, fulfillment director, transportation lead.
KPIs: Same-day ship %, cost/order, cycle time, pick accuracy, backlog aging, on-time delivery.
Depth: Shift diagnostics, facility comparison, carrier exception routing.
Costco-style · Membership + basket economics

Wholesale membership profitability dashboard

A dashboard connecting member renewal, traffic, basket structure, private label mix, gross margin, and club-level economics for executive review.

Audience: Country head, finance partner, store operations lead.
KPIs: Renewal %, trips/member, basket size, private label share, ancillary revenue, shrink/spoilage.
Depth: Renewal risk lens, club scorecards, category basket drill-through.

Include: Wireframe, KPI dictionary excerpt, model schema, release checklist.
